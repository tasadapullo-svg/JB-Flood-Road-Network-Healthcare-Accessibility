JB ANALYSIS GRID FREEZE v1.0
================================

STATUS: FROZEN

MASTER STUDY BOUNDARY
- JB_Study_Boundary_v1.0
- Johor Bahru District
- Source: MyGDI / JUPEM
- Metric CRS: EPSG:3377 (GDM2000 / Johor Grid)

COMMON GRID ORIGIN
- Origin X: -3000.000 m
- Origin Y: -84000.000 m
- Origin rule: floor(master boundary minimum x/y to the previous 1000 m)
- The same origin is used for ALL scales.

GRID SCALES
1. 500 m x 500 m = PRIMARY analysis grid
2. 250 m x 250 m = sensitivity grid
3. 1000 m x 1000 m = sensitivity grid

GRID ID
- 500 m: JB0500_R####_C####
- 250 m: JB0250_R####_C####
- 1000 m: JB1000_R####_C####
- Rows increase south -> north.
- Columns increase west -> east.
- IDs are permanent and must not be renumbered after flood evidence is added.

EDGE-CELL POLICY
- The analytical square cell remains the permanent grid unit.
- A clipped geometry is stored separately for exact calculations inside the study boundary.
- coverage_frac records the fraction of each square inside Johor Bahru District.
- is_edge = 1 when a cell is partially intersected by the study boundary.

FLOOD EVIDENCE POLICY
No historical flood severity or recurrence class is encoded in Step 2.
Future 2022 / 2023 / 2025 and multi-year evidence will be joined to these permanent Grid_ID values.

FUTURE FLOOD MAP STYLE
- Severe = red, 30% opacity
- Recurrent = yellow, 30% opacity
- Mild = blue, 30% opacity
Display priority: Severe > Recurrent > Mild
