STEP 3B POLYGON DELINEATION HIERARCHY v0.1

The polygons are evidence-support geometries, not modeled hydraulic inundation extents.

Priority:
A. OSM_NAMED_RESIDENTIAL_POLYGON
   Exact named mapped residential polygon; HIGH confidence.

B. OSM_RESIDENTIAL_POLYGON_CONTAINING_OFFICIAL_POINT
   Mapped residential polygon containing the independently resolved place coordinate;
   MEDIUM confidence; proxy for the local settlement support area.

B. OSM_NAMED_ROAD_CORRIDOR_WITHIN_FROZEN_500M_GRID
   Actual named OSM road segment(s) intersecting the location's permanent 500 m evidence
   cell. The closed polygon follows the road centerline using OSM width, lanes, or road-class
   defaults. MEDIUM confidence; represents the road-impact support corridor, not water extent.

C. FROZEN_500M_GRID_SUPPORT
   Used only when no defensible settlement polygon is available. LOW confidence.
   The already-frozen primary grid cell is retained rather than inventing a neighbourhood
   boundary with a circular buffer.

No manually drawn polygon and no D09-derived boundary is allowed.
