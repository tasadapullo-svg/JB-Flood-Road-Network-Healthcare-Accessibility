FLOOD-ZONE MAP DISPLAY RULE v1.0

The colours are fixed for future GIS figures:
- Severe flood zone: RED, 30% fill opacity.
- Recurrent flood zone: YELLOW, 30% fill opacity.
- Mild flood zone: BLUE, 30% fill opacity.

Display priority:
SEVERE > RECURRENT > MILD.

Important:
Severity and recurrence are stored as separate analytical attributes.
The priority rule affects only the final map display so one polygon is not ambiguously coloured.
No red/yellow/blue internal polygons are created before historical flood evidence is spatially validated.
