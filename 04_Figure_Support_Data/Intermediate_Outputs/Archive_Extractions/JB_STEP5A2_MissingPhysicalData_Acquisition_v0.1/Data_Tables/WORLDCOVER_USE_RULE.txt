WorldCover use rule

Primary variable:
    built_up_fraction_500m

WorldCover class 50 is 'Built-up' and includes buildings, roads and other man-made structures.
It is therefore used as a built-up fraction proxy.

Do NOT rename this variable to exact 'impervious surface percentage' in the manuscript.
A separate imperviousness product would be required for that stronger terminology.

The 2021 v200 map is frozen as the representative land-cover epoch.
No F01–F19 information is used to choose or alter this layer.
