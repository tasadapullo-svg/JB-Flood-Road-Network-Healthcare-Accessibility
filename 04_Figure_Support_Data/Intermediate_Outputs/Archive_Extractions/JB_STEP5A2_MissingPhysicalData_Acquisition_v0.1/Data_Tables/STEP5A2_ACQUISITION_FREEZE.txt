STEP 5A-2 ACQUISITION FREEZE

Allowed:
- acquire independent physical/environmental source data;
- clip to the already frozen study extent;
- verify file integrity and spatial coverage.

Not allowed:
- inspect F01–F19 to decide which source looks best;
- tune rainfall period or built-up definition against flood-zone hit rate;
- use D09 classes as predictor targets;
- discard a physical source because it lowers apparent validation performance.

Step 5A-3 may begin only after:
1. long-term rainfall raw/subset data are actually acquired;
2. WorldCover built-up layer is acquired;
3. baseline hydrography is extracted;
4. drainage/tide availability is explicitly resolved as acquired or documented unavailable/optional.
