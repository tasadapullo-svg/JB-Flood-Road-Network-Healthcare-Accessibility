# Known Limitations

1. The analysis is an event-based March 2025 study.
2. Exact road-closure onset and clearance times are incomplete.
3. Closure spatialization includes predefined uncertainty.
4. Population origins are spatially dependent.
5. D06R reference speeds are reference/free-flow speeds.
6. Flood susceptibility screening is not calibrated probability.
7. CRPI is a preparedness screening index.
8. Some large upstream datasets are externally referenced rather than committed.
