STEP 3B CLASSIFICATION RULE v0.1

Purpose:
Create the first closed GIS evidence-support polygons for the 16 recurrent locations
that occur in at least two of the 2022/2023/2025 core years.

D09 exclusion:
D09 susceptibility is NOT used in delineation or classification.

Display classes:
1. SEVERE (red, 30% opacity)
   Triggered only by high-consequence historical evidence:
   - evacuation / rescue;
   - trapped vehicle;
   - formal road closure / impassability;
   - or an independently documented high-depth/high-household-impact case.
2. RECURRENT (yellow, 30% opacity)
   At least two of the three core years, without a Step 3B severe trigger.
3. MILD (blue, 30% opacity)
   Style is frozen, but no location in this recurrent-only subset qualifies as MILD.
   No blue polygon is fabricated merely to complete the colour legend.

Important:
Frequency and severity remain separate analytical attributes. The final_class field is a
map-display class using the frozen priority Severe > Recurrent > Mild.
