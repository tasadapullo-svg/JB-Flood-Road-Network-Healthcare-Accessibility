METADATA QC WARNING

The MyGDI/JUPEM district feature used in the existing project asset reports:
- FCD = DA0085
- FNM = "District or Jajahan Coverage Administrative"

Under MS 1759 terminology, DA0080 is normally District/Jajahan Coverage Administrative,
whereas DA0085 denotes District/Jajahan Coverage Land.

This inconsistency is retained as a metadata warning and is NOT silently corrected.
The v1.0 spatial decision therefore relies on:
1) NAM = JOHOR BAHRU;
2) KOD_NEGERI = 01 and KOD_DAERAH = 02;
3) Sumber = JUPEM;
4) geometry agreement with DOSM ADM2;
5) near-complete agreement with the union of the 8 MyGDI subdistrict units.

The FCD field is not used as the sole identifier for the study boundary.
