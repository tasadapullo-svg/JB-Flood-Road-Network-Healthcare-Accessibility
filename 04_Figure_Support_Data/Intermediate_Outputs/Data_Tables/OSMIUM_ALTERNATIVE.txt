OSMIUM ALTERNATIVE

If pyrosm installation is inconvenient, use osmium-tool:

osmium tags-filter malaysia-singapore-brunei-261003.osm.pbf ^
  w/waterway=river,stream,canal,drain,ditch ^
  w/natural=coastline ^
  -o JB_hydrography_source.osm.pbf --overwrite

Then open the filtered PBF in QGIS and clip it using JB_Study_Boundary_v1.0.

This route is equivalent at source-selection level; exact layer conversion is frozen in Step 5A-3.
