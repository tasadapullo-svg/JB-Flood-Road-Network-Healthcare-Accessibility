7. INTERPRETATION
The effect class is descriptive, not a p-value.
100-m population cells are spatially dependent and should not be treated as 75,149 independent random observations.

A small citywide mean effect can still support a strong resilience paper if disruption is concentrated
in identifiable subdistricts or creates meaningful tail-risk / loss of reachability.

8. GRAPH IMPLEMENTATION

Partial boundary subedges are topologically blocked across the flood interval.

to the open/closed side of the boundary interval and the one-way direction.
