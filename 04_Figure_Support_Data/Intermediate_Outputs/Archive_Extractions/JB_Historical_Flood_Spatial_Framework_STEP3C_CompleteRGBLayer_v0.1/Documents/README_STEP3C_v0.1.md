## F02/F08/F16 review
No authoritative or exact named vector community boundary was found that could safely replace
the frozen 500 m evidence-support cells. They remain LOW confidence rather than being
artificially upgraded.

## Scientific interpretation
These polygons are historical evidence-support zones, not hydraulic flood-water extents.
The Mild class describes the documented historical event evidence available for the zone;
it does not mean the area can never experience a more severe future event.

## Freeze status
Classification/style structure is complete, but the full layer remains v0.1 because four
LOW-confidence geometries remain. D09 should still NOT be introduced until this layer is
accepted as the historical-evidence baseline.
